Evaluation audit — October 2026

Historical MLflow runs recorded mean R² ≈ 0.8815 using county-held-out 5-fold cross-validation with a LightGBM/Random Forest ensemble. A previously displayed 0.6440 value was traced to a hard-coded dashboard constant and could not be tied to an evaluation run, so it is no longer reported as model performance.

A later audit also identified an index-alignment bug in the out-of-fold prediction writeback path. The bug affected stored predictions, residuals, and downstream anomaly counts, but not the fold-level CV arrays used to calculate R². As a result, the previously reported 31-anomaly / 0.45% figure has been withdrawn pending a canonical rerun.

A later walk-forward time-CV run recorded mean R² ≈ 0.8483; it is a separate evaluation and is not combined with the historical county-CV result.

Presented TerraCast to Corteva representatives at the Data + AI Summit 2026.

Product story & engineering details

From scattered logs to a shared story

Care records and photos often live in separate places. I built Darin to connect quick daily logging with the family’s longer story: photo diaries, growth books, and shared moments. Book pages combine photos, diary text, and comments with templates and stickers.

Owning the app and its backend

Developed authentication, family roles and permissions, cross-device synchronization, and private photo/video access as the app moved from a local MVP to a server-backed service. I also built AI-assisted questions about care records and voice input. Iterated on real screens to fix duplicate controls, photo cropping, book spacing, and editing tools.

Maintainability and reliable saves

Split a 1,713-line editor into smaller modules, leaving a 205-line core module. Replaced 1,071 hard-coded UI strings while maintaining parity across Korean, English, Japanese, Spanish, and Simplified Chinese.

Test cases cover preserving drafts when a save fails, blocking duplicate saves, retrying failed requests, and preventing delayed saves from clearing a newer draft after navigation or account changes.

Water quality prediction

EY 2026 Challenge
Jan–Mar 2026

Integrated and validated 9,319 water-quality observations with 205 satellite, climate, hydrology, and geospatial features to predict alkalinity, electrical conductance, and dissolved reactive phosphorus in South African rivers.

My work: feature engineering, spatial cross-validation, and multi-stage modeling, with particular attention to the difficult phosphorus target.

Final leaderboard R²: 0.4259 · Rank: 66

What I learned from the experiments

Changes to the phosphorus prediction distribution moved the leaderboard score more than increasing model complexity. Spatial cross-validation and leaderboard results did not always agree. These observations came from competition feedback; they do not establish performance on a separate unseen dataset.

Class Imbalance in Image Data

UGA · Team study · 2024

We compared interpolation, Gaussian Pyramid, SRCNN, and wavelet resampling across GoogLeNet, ResNet50, AlexNet, and VGG16 on a seven-category sports-image dataset.

My work: experimental setup, data preprocessing, and SRCNN implementation. I also analyzed the results and helped draft and organize the paper.

SRCNN increased validation accuracy for AlexNet and VGG16 in our five-epoch experiments; GoogLeNet and ResNet50 did not improve.
